📋 Golden Rules

🔶 Before Every Response

  • SCROLL UP — read full chat history
  • Count ALL questions in the message
  • Check: Free or Paid? International?
  • Verify dates/details in CRM

🔷 Pause Handling

  • Member says pause = process it
  • Confirm exact dates + days
  • Fill DM form EVERY time
  • Share pause instructions after

  • Match member's language
  • Health = empathy FIRST
  • Use "we" not "I"
  • Acknowledge small things ("No worries")

  • Apologize → Screenshot → Troubleshoot
  • NEVER close until resolved
  • Repeated = escalate to team
  • International = already has 24hr access
