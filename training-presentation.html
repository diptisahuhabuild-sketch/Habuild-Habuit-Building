Step 6 — Retention

9 PM — Attended vs Missed Logic

Smart engagement paths based on today's attendance

✅

Attended Today

Positive Reinforcement
🎉 Congratulations + streak count
🔥 "X-day streak! Keep it going!"
📸 Shareable streak badge for social
🎁 Milestone rewards at Day 3, 7, 14, 21
😢

Missed Today

Re-Engagement
💬 "We missed you today!"
📅 Tomorrow's session time reminder
🔄 Option to switch batch timing
📞 After 3 missed → personal trainer call
💡 Key Insight: The 9 PM message is the most critical touchpoint — it determines next-day retention.
