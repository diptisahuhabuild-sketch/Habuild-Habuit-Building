Step 6 • Retention

9 PM — Attended vs Missed Logic

Smart engagement paths based on whether the user attended today's session

✅

Attended Today

Positive Reinforcement
🎉 Congratulations message with streak count
🔥 "You're on a X-day streak! Keep it going!"
📸 Shareable streak badge to post on social media
🎁 Milestone rewards at Day 3, 7, 14, 21
😢

Missed Today

Re-Engagement
💬 Empathetic message — "We missed you today!"
📅 "Tomorrow's session is at [time]. See you there?"
🔄 Option to switch batch timing if schedule doesn't fit
📞 After 3 missed days — personal call from trainer
💡 Key Insight: The 9 PM message is the most critical touchpoint — it determines next-day retention.
