❌ Here's What Actually Went Wrong

Actual Response (Real Chat)

"Sure, I'll pause your subscription. Take care and enjoy your trip! 😊"

🚫DM form not filled — subscription was paused but DM form was never submitted. This happened 8+ times across the team.
🚫Pause instructions not shared — member was never told the reactivation process or what happens after pause ends.
🚫24-hour access not mentioned — for international travel, we have 24-hour session access. Member could've continued attending!
🚫Timezone update not guided — should have shared Habuild app link + steps to update timezone for travel.
🚫Exact pause days not confirmed — "2 weeks" is vague. Should confirm exact dates and number of pause days.

Habuild
Topic: Pause Handling

✅ Standard Response — Step by Step

1
Empathize + Acknowledge
"We completely understand! We hope you have a wonderful trip."
2
Offer 24-hour access option
"Just so you know, for international travel we have 24-hour session access. You can join any batch using the same personal link from any timezone."
3
Guide timezone update
"You can update your timezone in the Habuild app: [Share app link] → Profile → Update Timezone."
4
Offer pause as backup
"However, if it's still not feasible, we can pause your subscription. Could you please confirm the exact dates (from–to) so we can process it?"
5
If paused → Share instructions + Fill DM form
Share complete pause instructions, confirm pause days, and FILL THE DM FORM.

Topic: Communication & Language

❌ Here's What Actually Went Wrong

Actual Response (Real Chat)

"I understand your concern. I will pause your subscription. Your subscription is valid till June 15th, 2026. Take care!"

🚫Replied in English when member wrote in Hindi — this is the #1 most repeated error across the team (50+ instances). Always respond in the member's language.
🚫Used "I" instead of "we" — we represent Habuild as a team, not as individuals. Always use "we".
🚫Empathy was generic — member mentioned health issue + doctor's advice. Needed more genuine concern.
🚫Pause dates not confirmed — didn't ask how long to pause or confirm exact dates.
⚠️ This error alone accounts for 50+ QC remarks. Language mismatch = member feels unheard.

✅ Standard Response — In Hindi/Hinglish

1
Respond in Hindi/Hinglish
"Namaste 🙏 Hum samajh sakte hain aapki situation. Hum hope karte hain aap jaldi theek ho jaayein."
2
Address health with empathy
"Please apna dhyan rakhein aur doctor ki advice follow karein. Aapki health sabse pehle hai."
3
Share subscription details
"Aapki subscription [date] tak valid hai."
4
Confirm pause dates
"Kya aap hume bata sakti hain ki aapko kitne din ke liye pause chahiye? Hum uske hisaab se process kar denge."
5
After confirming → Pause + DM form + Share instructions in Hindi
💡 Rules: Match member's language ALWAYS. Use "hum/we" not "main/I". Show genuine empathy for health. Confirm dates before pausing.

Actual Response (Real Chat)

🚫No apology first — member has been struggling for 2 days. Start with empathy and apology.

Topic: Link & Technical Issues

✅ Standard Response — Step by Step

1
Apologize + Empathize
"We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. We understand how frustrating this must be."
2
Share troubleshooting steps
"Please try turning off Restricted Mode on YouTube: [Steps]. Also, please try the alternate link: [Share alternate link]."
3
Ask for screenshot
"If the issue still persists, could you please share a screenshot of the error? We'll investigate further."
4
DO NOT give closure
Wait for member's confirmation. If issue persists after steps → escalate to concerned team.
💡 Rule: NEVER give closure on technical issues until confirmed resolved. Ask screenshot → try alternate → escalate.

Zero Tolerance

🚨 These Should NEVER Happen

❌ Sharing Paid Links to Free Members

Member was on expired free challenge but was shared a paid "Strong Everyday" recording link. Always check membership status first.

❌ Copy-Pasting ChatGPT Responses

Entire GPT-generated responses copied directly without editing or personalization. Always review, edit, and personalize before sending.

❌ Incorrect Information to Members

Wrong subscription dates, wrong plan details, wrong session timings shared. ALWAYS verify in CRM before responding.

❌ Responding Without Reading Chat History

Replying to queries already answered by another team member, or asking info that member already shared. SCROLL UP first.

Any of the above = immediate corrective action. No exceptions.
