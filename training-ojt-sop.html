5. OJT Onboarding & Process (45 Days)

Target: Minimum 80% readiness for independent handling with strong product/process understanding and consistent improvement.

Tracking Structure

All tracking in "OJT Leads Reporting Tracker" (separate sheets per batch). Daily updates: Chat Count (Support Master), Attendance (HR Sheet), Audit Scorecard (OJT Reporting), Feedback (OJT Personal Sheet).

OJT Lead Responsibilities

Daily Tracking & Audit

Track attendance, chat count, productivity. Audit 25–30% of chats (min 3x/week). Note feedback on batch docs under each intern name. Daily report in OJT group without reminders.

Feedback & QC

Share mistakes in group, one-on-one for each QC when needed (esp. initial days). Daily consolidated QC error feedback. Coordinate with QC team (weekly/15-day audits).

Refresher & Live Tests

Refresher sessions on error trends. Daily refresher sheet (topic, status, remarks, questions). Live chat tests: alternate days (W1–2) → 2x/day (W3–4) → 1x/week (W5–6).

Huddles & Support

10-min intern huddle at shift start. Help interns patiently. Morning/evening OJT lead alignment (5–10 min daily). Good coordination between leads for refreshers.

Evaluation & Training

Conduct OJT evaluations. When team strength low, help classroom training (material, sessions). Coordinate with Training Lead daily.

Audit Rotation (High Load)

Lead A: query support + escalation. Lead B: audits + feedback docs. Rotate every 1–2 hrs. New batch: prioritize floor support, adjust audit bandwidth.

Trend Logic

TrendCriteria (Priority: Error → Productivity → Others)
↑ ImprovingError % decreasing AND chat count increasing/stable
— StableError % and productivity roughly same (±2%)
↓ DecliningError % increasing OR chat count dropping without reason
⚠ At RiskError above threshold 2+ weeks OR attendance gaps OR behavioral concerns

Reporting

Daily OJT Report (WhatsApp group) | Weekly OJT Mail (management) | QC Audit Feedback (weekly/15-day) | Final Batch Report (end of 45 days, all stakeholders)

6. Retraining & OJT Extension

If significant performance gaps are observed:

Step 1: Handholding

Additional refresher sessions, focused improvement plan created

Step 2: Monitoring

Additional audits, closer monitoring, daily feedback

Step 3: Extension

OJT extended (max 2 additional weeks). Decision by Training Lead + OJT Lead + Dept Stakeholders jointly

Final transition decision based on demonstrated improvement and readiness.

7. OJT to Squad Transition

Final Review (End of 45 Days)

Final consolidated review with all weekly parameters + OJT dates, duration, shifting status (Ready/Not Ready/Exited), squad name, final department.

Transition Timeline

OJT (45 Days)

Min 80% readiness, strong product understanding, consistent improvement

Month 1 (Transition)

Shift to squad, mentor continues, gradual targets. Min 70% of squad benchmark

Month 2 (Stabilize)

Mentor as needed, productivity improving, error under control. 70%+ benchmark

Post Month 2

Same expectations as squad. Formal PIP applicable if required

Department Transition Guidelines

  • OJT Team and Department Leads coordinate continuously — all OJT parameters tracked
  • Department Leads support interns patiently — no negative influence
  • Gradual target progression maintained, escalations highlighted immediately
⛔
Any action against interns only after proper stakeholder discussion and as per OJT criteria.

9. Key Operational Guidelines

  • Intern tracking every working day without fail — data always updated and accurate
  • Professional behavior by all teams — communication transparent and documented
  • Feedback focused on improvement and readiness
  • OJT successful only when interns independently handle queries with required quality and productivity

Load Crisis Management

High volume: OJT Leads jointly support queries, audits prioritized by risk, refreshers moved to low-load windows, team coordination ensures quality and member experience maintained.
