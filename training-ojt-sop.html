Standard Operating Procedure — End to End

End-to-End Process Flow

Hiring→ Onboarding→ Classroom Training→ OJT (45 Days)→ Squad Transition

This SOP covers the complete journey from hiring to squad transition, ensuring every intern is systematically trained, tracked, and prepared for independent handling.

1. Hiring Process

HR team is responsible for sourcing, screening, and onboarding new hires. The following must be ensured before interns join training:

  • Strengthen LinkedIn and profile background screening during recruitment
  • Assess long-term career alignment during interviews — flag mismatches early
  • Flag candidates with repeated short tenures and frequent switches
  • Closely evaluate technical and specialized degree profiles for retention risk
  • Ensure all documentation is verified before joining date
  • Shift and department allocation finalized during or before classroom training period
  • Final allocation documented and communicated before OJT begins
  • Once finalized, allocation will generally remain frozen unless a business-critical requirement arises

2. Onboarding

On Day 1 of training, the following onboarding activities must be completed:

  • Welcome & HR Induction — Rules, regulations, free 14-day challenge registration
  • Habuild overview — company vision, departments, workflow
  • Performers interaction — growth story and motivation
  • Department lead introductions (scheduled across training days)
  • Training plan overview & expectation setting for the entire training period
  • Intern ID creation and system access setup
  • Yoga registration and wellness orientation

5. OJT Onboarding & Process

OJT duration is 45 days. Target: Make interns minimum 80% ready for independent query handling with strong product/process understanding, consistent quality, and productivity improvement.

OJT Tracking Structure

All tracking maintained in one workbook: "OJT Leads Reporting Tracker" with separate sheets per batch.

OJT Lead Responsibilities

  • Daily tracking of assigned interns — attendance, chat count, productivity
  • Review chats and provide actionable feedback
  • Conduct audits (minimum 25–30% of total chats) and maintain scorecards
  • Note all feedback on batch documents under each intern's name as per parameters
  • Share QC feedback with interns — mistakes in group, one-on-one when required (especially initial days)
  • Connect daily (or when required) to share consolidated QC error feedback
  • Conduct refresher sessions based on error trends
  • Update Daily Refresher Sheet with topic, status, remarks, and questions taken
  • Conduct Live Chat Tests (alternate days → twice/day → once/week)
  • Daily 10-min huddle with interns at shift start — recent updates, expected queries, faster solutions
  • Help interns with queries patiently with professional and positive behavior
  • Handle escalations and coordinate with Training Lead daily
  • Coordinate and conduct OJT Evaluations
  • When team strength is low, help in classroom training — material, sessions as per situation
  • OJT Leads must maintain good coordination with each other — especially for refresher sessions
  • Share daily work report in OJT group without reminders

6. Retraining & OJT Extension Process

If significant performance gaps are observed during OJT:

  • Additional handholding and focused refresher sessions will be conducted
  • A focused improvement plan will be created for the intern
  • Additional audits and closer monitoring will be done
  • OJT duration may be extended if required

OJT Extension Guidelines

  • Extension should generally not exceed 2 additional weeks
  • Extension decisions will be taken jointly by: Training Lead + OJT Lead + Department Stakeholders
  • Final transition decision based on demonstrated improvement and readiness

7. OJT to Squad Transition Framework

Final Review (End of 45 Days)

Final consolidated review prepared with all weekly scorecard parameters plus: OJT Start/End Date, Duration, Shifting Status (Ready/Not Ready/Exited), Squad Name, Final Department.

Transition Timeline

PhaseDurationExpectations
OJT Period45 DaysMinimum 80% readiness, strong product/process understanding, consistent quality & productivity improvement
Month 1 (Transition)After OJTShift to squad, dedicated mentor support continues, gradual productivity expectations, quality as primary focus. Min 70% of squad benchmark.
Month 2 (Stabilization)Month 2Mentor support as required, productivity continues improving, error % under control. 70%+ of squad benchmark.
Post Month 2OngoingSame productivity and quality expectations as squad members. Formal PIP applicability begins if required.

Department Transition Guidelines

  • OJT Team and Department Leads must coordinate continuously
  • All OJT parameters continue to be tracked during transition
  • Department Leads must support interns patiently — no negative influence
  • Gradual target progression as per OJT thresholds maintained
  • Escalations highlighted to Training Lead immediately
⛔
Important: Any action against interns must only be taken after proper stakeholder discussion and as per OJT criteria.

9. Key Operational Guidelines

  • Intern tracking must happen every working day without fail
  • Data entries must always remain updated and accurate
  • Professional behavior must be maintained by all teams
  • All communication must be transparent and documented
  • Feedback must focus on improvement and readiness
  • OJT is successful only when interns can independently handle queries with required quality and productivity

Load Crisis Management

During high member volume: OJT Leads jointly support query handling, audits prioritized by risk and bandwidth, refresher sessions moved to low-load windows, team coordination ensures neither quality nor member experience is impacted.
