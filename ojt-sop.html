Objective

To ensure systematic, transparent, and performance-driven tracking of interns during their 45-day OJT (On Job Training) period through:

📊

Daily Monitoring

Daily monitoring and updates by OJT Leads

📋

Weekly Scorecards

Weekly scorecard tracking and structured feedback

🤝

Cross-Team Coordination

Coordination between Training, OJT, QC, Calling, Support, and other departments

🎯

Final Review & Transition

Final consolidated review and department transition process

🏆
Goal: Prepare interns to become 95% ready for handling member queries independently while maintaining productivity and quality standards aligned with squad expectations.

QC & Audit Guidelines

RequirementCriteria
QC FrequencyMinimum 3 times per week per intern
Audit Coverage25–30% minimum of total chats
Audit ResponsibilityOJT Leads + Calling Leads + QC Team Support

Audit Guidelines

  • Daily audit scorecards must be maintained
  • Weekly consolidated scorecards must be updated
  • Feedback should be practical, actionable, and structured
  • Repeated errors should be tracked separately
  • Random re-audits may be conducted by Training Lead

Department Transition Guidelines

If interns are shifted immediately after classroom training to Calling Team or any other department:

Mandatory Coordination Requirements

  • OJT Team and Department Leads must coordinate continuously
  • All OJT parameters must continue to be maintained
  • Department attendance must be marked in the required sheets
  • Chat counts must be updated in the master sheets
  • Availability tracking must continue properly
  • Interns must attend all required refresher sessions and evaluations
  • Department Leads must connect with Training Lead weekly or whenever required
  • Escalations must immediately be highlighted to the Training Lead
H
HABUILD
14

Department Lead Responsibilities During OJT

  • Supporting interns patiently and professionally
  • Helping interns with doubts and live handling
  • Ensuring no wrong influence or negative environment impacts interns
  • Maintaining gradual target progression as per OJT thresholds
  • Conducting audits wherever required
  • Coordinating with QC Team during the 15-day review process
  • Ensuring interns attend important refresher sessions
  • Ensuring all evaluations are completed properly
  • Maintaining proper professional communication
⛔
Important: Any action against interns must only be taken after proper stakeholder discussion and as per OJT criteria.

Operational Guidelines

  • Intern tracking must happen every working day without fail
  • Data entries should always remain updated and accurate
  • Professional behavior should be maintained by all teams
  • All communication should remain transparent and documented
  • Feedback should focus on improvement and readiness
  • OJT is considered successful only when interns can independently handle queries confidently with required quality and productivity
