OJT Team SOP
Standard Operating Procedure
Objective

Ensure systematic tracking of interns during their 45-day OJT period to make them 95% ready for independently handling member queries with required quality and productivity standards.

Classroom Training → OJT (45 Days) → Department Transition
ℹ️
Outcomes: Intern is shifted to a department (Support / Calling / Other) or exited based on performance, discipline, or behavioral concerns.
OJT Tracking Structure

All tracking maintained in one workbook: "OJT Leads Reporting Tracker" with separate sheets per batch (Batch 10, 11, 12, etc.)

OJT Lead Responsibilities
  • Daily tracking of assigned interns — attendance, chat count, productivity
  • Review chats and provide actionable feedback
  • Conduct audits (minimum 25–30% of total chats) and maintain scorecards
  • Note all feedback and suggestions on batch documents under each intern's name as per the parameters
  • Share QC feedback with interns — mistakes discussed in group, and one-on-one when required for each QC (especially in initial days when interns need more guidance)
  • Connect once a day (or when required) to share consolidated QC error feedback with the team
  • Conduct refresher sessions based on error trends
  • Update Daily Refresher Sheet with topic, status, and remarks (see below)
  • Conduct Live Chat Tests as per the schedule below
  • Daily 10-min huddle with interns at start of shift — share recent updates, common queries expected for the day, and how to solve them faster with quality
  • Help interns with their queries patiently while maintaining professional and positive behavior
  • Handle escalations and coordinate with Training Lead daily
  • Monitor intern discipline, professionalism, and communication
  • Coordinate and conduct OJT Evaluations as per schedule
  • When team strength is low, help in classroom training — prepare training material, take sessions, etc. as per the situation
  • OJT Leads must maintain good coordination with each other for everything — especially refresher sessions. Both leads must be fully updated and take sessions in a consistent, high-quality way
  • Share daily work report in OJT group without reminders

Live Chat Test Schedule

OJT Leads act as members — they message from their own number to the support number. Interns must handle the queries as real chats. Questions must be based on topics covered in refresher sessions.

OJT PhaseFrequencyPurpose
Start (Week 1–2)Alternate daysBuild confidence, test basic topic understanding
Mid (Week 3–4)Twice a dayTest speed + accuracy under real-like conditions
Last (Week 5–6)Once a weekFinal readiness check — intern should handle independently
📌
All questions asked during live chat tests must be recorded in the Daily Refresher Sheet under "Questions Taken" so performance can be tracked against specific topics.
Morning & Evening Team Alignment — OJT Lead Huddle

Both Morning and Evening OJT Leads must conduct a 5–10 minute huddle to stay aligned on intern progress, refresher topics, live chat test observations, and any concerns. Frequency: Daily (preferred), or weekly / as required based on workload. This ensures consistent training quality across both shifts.

Daily Report Must Include

Team count • Chat count by team • Audit count • Refresher conducted (1-line feedback) • Live chat tests taken (count + topic) • Escalations handled • Sheet updates done • Availability/productivity concerns with remarks if targets are low.

Department Transition Guidelines

When interns are shifted to a department during or after OJT:

  • OJT Team and Department Leads must coordinate continuously
  • All OJT parameters (attendance, chat count, availability) must continue to be tracked
  • Interns must attend all refresher sessions and evaluations
  • Department Leads must connect with Training Lead weekly
  • Department Leads must support interns patiently — no negative influence
  • Gradual target progression as per OJT thresholds must be maintained
  • Escalations must be highlighted to Training Lead immediately
⛔
Important: Any action against interns must only be taken after proper stakeholder discussion and as per OJT criteria.
Refresher Sessions & Escalation Handling
Refresher Sessions

Conducted based on: error trends, productivity concerns, repeated audit issues, escalation observations. Reports must include session name, attendance, 1-line feedback, key observations, and improvement areas.

Escalation Handling

Major concerns (performance, behavior, attendance, professionalism, negative influence) must be immediately escalated to: Training Lead → Relevant Stakeholders → HR Team (if required).

Key Operational Guidelines
  • Intern tracking must happen every working day without fail
  • Data entries must always remain updated and accurate
  • Professional behavior must be maintained by all teams
  • All communication must be transparent and documented
  • Feedback must focus on improvement and readiness
  • OJT is successful only when interns can independently handle queries with required quality and productivity
