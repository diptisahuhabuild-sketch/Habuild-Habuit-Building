Observations During Training

Positive Observations

Good English communication — this batch overall has good English communication skills compared to previous batches. All members showed good engagement during sessions and activities, actively participating in discussions and energizers.

Areas of Concern

Activity participation barrier: 2–3 interns (Mehak, Pretty, Pareedhi) are not comfortable participating in any type of group activity, which creates a barrier in building communication confidence. Pareedhi additionally does not sound attentive mindfully — seems blank even for basic things. Feedback has been shared for the same and close observation will continue throughout OJT.

Critical Observation — Typing Speed

Important: During mentor assignment and OJT start, it was observed that few interns' typing speed is very slow. Since our process is chat-based, typing speed plays a major role in productivity. Even after spending much time on training and support, slow typing leads to low chat count and becomes a significant bottleneck. This should be analysed and addressed early in OJT to avoid long-term productivity issues.
